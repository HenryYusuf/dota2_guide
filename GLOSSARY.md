# Dota 2 Companion App

An unofficial, offline-first Dota 2 reference app: browse heroes, items, and patch notes without a network connection. Not affiliated with Valve Corporation.

## Language

**Companion App**:
An unofficial third-party app that presents Dota 2 reference data; it is not part of the game and never talks to the game client.
_Avoid_: helper, assistant

**Hero**:
A playable character, defined by attributes, combat stats, abilities, talents, and facets.
_Avoid_: character, champion

**Ability**:
A power a hero possesses; basic, ultimate, or innate, optionally with an Aghanim's Scepter/Shard enhancement.
_Avoid_: spell, skill

**Innate Ability**:
An ability a hero has from the start of the game without spending ability points.
_Avoid_: passive ability

**Talent**:
One of a hero's eight level-gated bonus choices, arranged as four tiers (levels 10, 15, 20, 25) with two options each.
_Avoid_: talent tree node, bonus

**Facet**:
A hero variant chosen when the hero is picked, which changes how some of the hero's abilities work.
_Avoid_: variant, loadout

**Aghanim's Scepter / Shard**:
Ability enhancements granted by the items Aghanim's Scepter and Aghanim's Shard.
_Avoid_: aghs upgrade

**Item**:
A purchasable object with a cost, attributes, and active or passive effects.
_Avoid_: gear, equipment

**Recipe**:
The component list and combination cost that produces a higher-tier item.
_Avoid_: crafting

**Patch**:
A numbered game update (e.g. 7.41f) together with its structured change notes.
_Avoid_: version, hotfix

## Deferred content concepts

**Guide**:
An authored educational article about playing Dota, optionally followed by a quiz. Not in v1.
_Avoid_: tutorial, walkthrough. Never used to mean a hero's recommended build.

**Build**:
A curated recommendation of which abilities to level and which items to buy on a hero. Distinct from Guide. Not in v1.
_Avoid_: hero guide
